I'm a Software Engineer living and working in Portland, Oregon.

As a technologist, I find joy in building tools that solve problems and / or enable fun. I've spent most of my career working in closed-source enterprise environments, but lately I'm making an endeavor to contribute to and create Open Source projects, mostly via GitHub.
